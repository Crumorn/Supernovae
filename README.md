This is a mod for the 1997 Video Game *Emperor of the Fading Suns*, inspired by concepts from the [Nova mod](https://web.archive.org/web/20180723011811/http://website.lineone.net/~rwein/nova/nova.htm), with many of our own ideas added on top, but instead for the 2025 re-release of the game.

With the 2025 Enhanced edition of *Emperor of the Fading Suns*, we now have a playable, if not outright complete, version of the game. Unlike when the Nova mod was being worked on between 1997 and 1999. However, almost anyone who has played a modified video game in the last 15 years could tell you that the point of modding shouldn't be to "fix", or make the game playable in first place. Instead ideally one would buy a complete title, and mod it to have the game suite your tastes, or expand upon and polish weaker aspects of the game. It can be said, certain parts of the vanilla experience do leave some room for improvement, parts that Nova had improved upon. However the latest version of Nova was released in 1999, and so inherently much of what Nova adds was balanced around an older version of the game, and while Nova is still an interesting addition to 2025 EFS, it doesn't actually add that much in comparison to modern mods like Hyperion or Emperor Wars. The issues I personally take with those mods however, is that Hyperion takes place in A.D. 6000(not the setting I'm looking for) and Emperor Wars presents, to me at least, conflicting game design philosophies by granting you an advanced start with at least 3 planets per House, but also a very stunted start, with most early game tech, and build options offering little to nothing. This of course ignores the most glaring issue with the latest versions of Emperor Wars, that of course being its egregious use of stylistically incompatible AI art that white washes certain house members... Suffice to say, Supernovae seeks to be a spiritual successor to Nova that lays in-between Nova and Emperor wars in it's depth, while adhering to a no AI policy, and strives to remain stylistically consistent with EFS.
