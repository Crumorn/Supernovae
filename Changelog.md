
# Supernovae Version 0.1

#### Field Guns
 - Reduced move speed of all field guns to 0
 - Made converted move type to foot (this allows medics with move type foot to heal field guns)

#### Nobles
  - Removed dueling from all nobles unless otherwise specified
  - Added House Specific Nobles. Each house gains the 3 nobles shown during portrait selection plus 2 other nobles with random names. It was difficult quickly finding anything lore accurate for the nobles, so the following is improvised. A note should be made to come back later and update the house specific nobles to be lore friendly.
    - Li Halan
        - Flavius and Bladmaster Flavius
            - Behaves Like Vanilla Noble, except does not have dueling before upgrade.
        - Celestra and Theurgist Celestra
            - Has Weaker physical stats but powerful psionics. Requires Theurgy to upgrade. Theurgist cersion counts as Clergy Member. Gains dueling on upgrade.
        - Chi Kao and Blademaster Chi Kao
            - Has slightly weaker physcial stats that are supllmented by decent psionics. Requires both Blademaster and Theurgy Tech. Gains Dueling on Upgrade.
        - Ikoma and Bishop Ikoma
            - Significantly worse combat stats, but upgraded version acts like clergy and medic, and can attack on B2 (like a spy). Requires meditation to upgrade.
        - Zheng and Cardinal Zheng
            - Terrible combat stats for a noble, but upgraded version acts like clergy, medic, and relic, and can attack on B2 (like a spy) and is immune to plague. Unsure how giving him relic status will work in game.
    - The Hazat 
        - Given their militartist Nature, all Hazat have the Dueling Ability
        - Lucinda and Blademaster Lucinda
            - Trades armor for high agility, spot, and move. Does less close in favor of having a indirect attack. Concept being Lucinda is better at spotting the enemy and calling in artillery.
        - Catherine and Blademaster Catherine
            - Has high camo and slightly buffed spot, and trades weapon strength for higher accuracy. Strong vs high agility low armor units. 
        - Lazurus and Blademaster Lazurus
            - Sacrifices mobility and agility for high armor, trades accuracy for weapon strength. Strong vs low agility high armor.
        - Juan and Juan Blademaster
            - Acts as Vanilla
        - Piscia and Piscia Blademaster
            - Slightly decreased attack strength, acts as medic.
    - House Decados
        - All Decados nobles can attack on B2 and never rebel
        - Nicolai and Fleshcrafter Nicolai
            - Base Nicolai has same combat stats as base noble with slightly improved camo. Fleshcrafter Nicolai requires combat drugs to upgrade, has imporved stats over a blademaster, but suffers draw backs from using performence enhancing drugs. Requires Combat Drugs to Upgrade.
        - Hyram and Blademaster Hyram
            - Acts as Vanilla with slightly improved camo
        - Salandra and Jakovian Salandra
            - Base Salandra acts has the same combat states as a normal noble. Jakovian Salandra gains an immense spot and camo to represent her being apart of the Jakovian society. Requires Psychosocial manipualtion to upgrade.
        - Angelika and Commissar Angelika
            - Slightly reduced combat attack strength. Commisar Angelika can however, tow, act as a officer and a medic, and can refuel V/STOL aicraft. This is to represent a hidden logistics network and whipping conscripts into shape, for morale. She requires composit armor to upgrade.
        - Elena and Cloak Elena
            - Base Elena has a higher camo skill than the average unit, but is otherwise unremarkable. When upgraded to Cloak Elena she gains a high camo, high spot, and has the tactical plague ability. Requires doppleganger and pesulator bio-artillary tech's to upgrade.
            - At the onset may need nerffed, I basically meshed the Blademaster and Doppleganger stats and gave it plague.
            - Should act like spy AND Noble
    - House Hawkwood
        - Alexius and Blademaster Alexius
            - Acts like vanilla with slightly improved combat stats.
        - Alvarex, Pilot Alverax, Blademaster Alverax, Master Pilot Alverax
            - Has vanilla noble stats but can be put into an atmos fighter at a factory. Concept being a fighter pilot Ace
        - Cedrick and Admiral Cedrick
            - Cedrick has the same base noble stats, Admiral Cedrick has slighty better spot and attack strength.
            - Admiral Cedrick can by built into a Frigate, Destroyer, Cruiser, Dreadnought, or Carrier provided the appropriate tech is available. Each of these ships are meant to act like better variants or flagships that Cedrick commands.
        - Saphira and Tank Commander Saphira
            - Base Saphira is underpowered for a noble with a large reduction to their combat stats across the board. She can be upgraded into Tank commander Saphira given assault tanks have been researched. Tank Commander Saphira  has stats closer to a baseline Noble, acts like a medic, and can be upgraded at a factory into either an Improved Assault Tank or a Improved Hover Tank. Both tank varients act like Medics to represent Saphira's engineering skills.
